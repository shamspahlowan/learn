I'm a Flutter developer moving into machine learning engineering. Courses and playlists are everywhere, but they don't add up to a plan, and they don't make me prove I understood anything.

So I'm building this site. It has twenty courses, from linear algebra to ML system design. Every lesson is tagged by depth:

- **Core**: what a working ML engineer must be able to do
- **Interview**: what I must be able to explain or derive under pressure
- **Research**: the proofs and papers behind it

Each lesson links to the best resources I could find, from official course pages and books to the picks that come up again and again on Reddit and GitHub. I write my own notes as I go, and those notes are the real output.

Short animations made with [Manim](https://docs.manim.community/) will follow for the ideas that are easier to see than to read.

The first lesson with notes is [Dot product and cosine similarity](/learn/courses/linear-algebra/dot-product-and-cosine-similarity/). It's the operation behind embedding search and attention.
